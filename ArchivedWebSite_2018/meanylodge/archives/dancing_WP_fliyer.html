COMBINE FOLK DANCING WITH AN INEXPENSIVE MOUNTAIN WEEKEND

Every Other Weekend Starting Sept 12th
Folk Dance Evenings at Meany Lodge

This is a chance to visit a ski lodge where folk dancing is a big part of the schedule. Enjoy Meany's pre-season work parties. Make a lot of wonderful friends. Enjoy the non-polluted, non-smoking and non-alcoholic surroundings. Work parties are a great way to meet people and the quickest way to become a part of the Meany "family".

You might even learn new skills as we get the lodge ready for the winter season. This fall we are busy remodeling the lodge's kitchen, chopping wood, painting, marking cross-country trails, repairing bridges, and installing another rope tow in the downhill area.

Not that it's all work. You'll find time for mountain hikes, moonlight strolls, nature photography, exploring our own waterfalls, and picking wild berries. If you don't want to stay in the lodge there are plenty of places just perfect for pitching a tent.

To Sign up: Call the Mountaineers, 284-8484. For more information, call Ray or Phyllis Nelson, 885-0693, or Linda Boscarine, 248-0222; or Christina Roseman, 644-7354. Work parties are scheduled for Sept 12-13, Sept 26-27, Oct. 10-11, Oct. 24-25, Nov. 7-8, and Nov. 21-22.

What to Bring: Your dancing shoes, sleeping bag, flashlight, gloves & work clothes. Don't forget that late in the season the mountains can get chilly so you will want to dress accordingly. Meals for an entire weekend cost about $10 and are prepared by experienced volunteer cooks. These can include Saturday lunch and dinner, as well as Sunday's breakfast and lunch.

How to Get There: Take I-90 east approximately 10 miles beyond Snoqualmie Pass. Take Exit 62. Turn right. Follow F.S. Rd. 54 1.6 mi. Turn left. Follow F.S. Rd. 420 1.7 mi. to end. Park at R/R tracks. Walk a short distance uphill (on the road) to lodge.

For Long Distance Planners: Don't forget that Meany Ski Lodge will be open for downhill, telemarking and cross country skiing from Dec. 26th through Jan. 4th. And, downhill lessons (both children and adults sections) start Jan. 10th.
