1986 Workparty Schedule


Want a super unforgettable bargain? Meany Workparites are a great way to meet new friends, and get a good workout, and enjoy free lodging and terrific meals at cost. Sing up with the Clubroom for meals.

WORKPARTY SCHEDULE

  • September 7th - 8th: Share YOUR Summer Adventures
    Bring prints slides, or gift of gab.
  • September 20th - 21th: "Earthquake Hazards in the NW" - Steve Malone
  • October 4th - 5th: Grand Canyon Thrills - Ken & Carol Nielson
    See shots from a river run and a hroseback trip down the canyon's steep sides
  • October 18th - 19th: Mexican weekend - len & Linda Boscarine
    This weekend includes "Buid a Taco", a Piñata, Mexican Dances, and a view of Mexico
  • November 1st - 2nd: Steak feed - Final Workparty

WORKPARTY PROJECTS

  • Finish wiring hill lights
  • Plant trees, brush hill, gather firewood
  • Specialized building and repair projects
  • Sno-cat and Pak-rat need to be prepared for ski season

WHAT TO BRING
Come with your workclothes, gloves, sleeping bag, pack, and flashlight. Bring dance shoes for weekly Saturday night folkdances.

DIRECTIONS
Take I-90 east 10 miles past Snoqualmie Pass. Take Exit 62, turn right. Follow F.S. Rd. 54 1.6 miles. Turn left, follow F.S. Rd 420 1.7 miles to end. Park at R/R tracks. Walk short distance to lodge
