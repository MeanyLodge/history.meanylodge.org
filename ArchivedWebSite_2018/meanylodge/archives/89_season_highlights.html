89 Season Highlights

  1. 67 people completed the Alpine and Cross-Country Ski Courses. The following is a summary of the Meany 1989 Ski Courses. The enrichment add-ons are also listed.
    • Two four-week sessions of PSIA-NW recognized Ski School under the direction of Patti Polinsky Claar, certified Ski instructor
    • One three week Cross-Country Ski Course was held in February
    • A Telemark Class was added to Cross-country offerings
    • In February, a Weekend clinic on gates and ski-racing was taught in February
  2. First Aid Courses at Meany catered to 44 people. These included one MOFA and two MOFA Refresher courses.
  3. Meany lodged capacity crowds until March when ski lessons were over.
  4. The 1989 season contained an unusually large number of good snow days making excellent skiing on ungroomed hills. However, February temperatures were very cold.
  5. Two aesthetically attractive concrete block houses were built to encase gas tanks above ground as the risk of rupture of underground tanks was considered too great.
  6. A septic system was installed. This involved putting in a 4,000 gallon tank and accompanying drain field
  7. Modifications were made to the Super Worm Tow.
  8. Meany's new third rope tow received lots of use.
  9. Meany's remodeled kitchen was given a workout and many accolades.
  10. To expand the use of Meany Hut a once-a-month Friday night opening was tried.
  11. Outstanding Saturday evening presentations and folkdancing with live music entertained crowds. Bridge games enticed some people.
  12. Meany Advertising Committee continued to make presentations to community groups, at Mountaineer orientation meetings, and at ski shows.
