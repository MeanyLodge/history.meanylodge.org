Annual Bike & Barbeque Bash

1989 NICKELL'S ANNUAL
Bike ’n’ Barbeque Bash

WHEN: June 25, I989
WHAT:A BBQ and bicycle tour of the spectacular attractions on Bainbridge
LOCATION:BBQ: 11924 Miller Rd. NE
Bike Tour starts at Winslow Ferry Terminal
FARE:Beene Bread and Beverages will be provided. Bring your own “throw on the BBQ fare”
TOUR:Ferry leaving Seattle 10:10 AM will be met by guide at 11:00 AM
NOTE:For those who can’t/don’t bike, transportation can be arranged
Call 842-3004 to RSVP

DUE NOTE: Norm Vigus has suggest somewhat more streneous bike routes to the BBQ

Option1: Bike to Tacoma,
  • Over Tacoma Narrows Bridge.
  • Bike fifty miles up SR 16 to SR 305.
  • Turn right and proceed eight miles to Day Road
  • Turn left on to Day Road go abouts3/4 of a mile
  • Turn left on to Lovgreen Road and up the steep driveway.
  • Go to the houee with the Chrietmae tree lights.
Option 2: Take any route towards Anacortes.
  • Take SR 20 onto Whidbey Island
  • Go to Keystone Ferry Terminal
  • Take ferry to Port Townsend
  • At Port Townsend turn left onto SR 20
  • Follow SR 20 To SR 101
  • Take 101 to 104 and cross Hood Canal Bridge
  • Bear right to get onto SR 3
  • Turn left onto SR 305
  • Proceed 8 milee to Day Road
  • Turn left on to Day Road go abouts3/4 of a mile
  • Turn left on to Lovgreen Road and up the steep driveway.
  • Go to the houee with the Chrietmae tree lights.
Once you get to house, please leave bikes and other heavy machinery outside.
