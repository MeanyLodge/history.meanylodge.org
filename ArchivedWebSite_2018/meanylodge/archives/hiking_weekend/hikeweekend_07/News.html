Added June 27, 2007

Dog sledding demonstration

Michael and Doreen Johnston will bring their sled dogs to Meany on Saturday, August 4, for a special afternoon/evening program. Saturday afternoon after the hike, they will put the team to work and demonstrate summer pulling. After dinner, they will give a presentation in the lodge about dog sledding.

What a treat to have Michael and Doreen come to Family Hiking Weekend and bring their dog team. This will be a great opportunity for us to learn about this winter recreation with which we share the forests and trails around Meany.

The Johnston's and their dogs will stay near the telephone cabin, the forested area below the tow hut away from the main lodge area. For more information about the program, check out our Dog sled program page.
