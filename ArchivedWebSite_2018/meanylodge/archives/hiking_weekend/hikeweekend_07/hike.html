Annual Family Snoqualmie Peaks and Lakes weekend

Come join us at Meany Lodge for an adventure filled weekend of Hiking, Climbing, Kite Flying, mountaineer camaraderie and outdoor fun, August 2nd and 3rd, 2003.



Meany Lodge will be adventure central for a weekend of exciting outdoor activities for all ages. The Lodge will be organizing hikes, scrambles and climbs in the Stampede and Snoqualmie Pass areas, kite flying, lawn games, and of course we save the best for last - great food.

Hikes, scrambles and climbs are planned for both days. Included in the selection will be hikes suitable for children as young as 2 years old on Meany Lodge property, or as long as 2, 4, 7, or 13 miles on thePacific Crest Trail. Hikes along the Crest Trail end on a hillside of wild strawberries. A shuttle to the trailhead will be provided for those hiking the Pacific Crest Trail. There will be hikes to mountain lakes both days. Bring your fishing pole. We are planning to offer many of the Snoqualmie 1st and 2nd 10 Pin Peaks. A full list of hikes, scrambles and climbs will be available soon.

After a day of hiking or climbing, return to the Lodge to clean up and relax with friends. Kite making and flying, a kite flying lecture and lawn games will be available in the late afternoon for our younger guests.

An after dinner a slide show will be followed by coffee and a special evening sweet treat. Looking at the night sky for meteorite showers and shooting stars will be the final evening’s entertainment.

You may sleep in the lodge, in a tent or even under the stars with the lodge facilities available to you all night. There is limited self-contained motor home parking about 200 yards from the lodge.

Morning will come early. After eating breakfast and making your own sack lunch (everything will be provided), we will head off for another day of adventure in the area.

Note: Complete details will be sent via email or posted to those who sign-up.

Cost: Adults: $25; Children: 6 - 13 $10; Under 6: free. Includes: Kite, overnight accommodation, afternoon snacks, a scrumptious 3 course dinner, (including a delicious vegetarian entree), cooked breakfast, and a sack lunch for Sunday’s activities.

Make your reservations with the clubroom. Then contact Joanne de Lautour (email: Roving_Kiwi@hotmail.com), Patti Polinsky (email: MrBunz11@aol.com or 206/525 7464) or Chuck Welter (email: ecwelter@hotmail.com or 253/926-0867) for further details of the weekend.

Selected Hikes from Meany Lodge for the 2003 Hiking Weekend


Check the Mountaineer Magazine for more technical climbs sponsored by Scrambles and Climbing committees.
Saturday Meeting at Meany Lodge

Meet at Meany Lodge on Saturday at 10 am with your sack lunch.
1. Cottonwood and Mirror Lakes - Approximately 0.7 mile to Cottonwood and 0.4 miles further to Mirror lake. Beyond Mirror lake is a beautiful panorama of Lost Lake Basin. This is a lovely circular trail with no backtracking. Total mileage for the circular trip is 2.1.miles. The total elevation gain either route is 640 feet. This is a good childrens’ trail. The trailhead is near Lost Lake, which is approximately 4 miles from the Lodge. You will need a trailhead-parking permit.
2. Stirrup Lake (Meadow Pass) to the Lodge. A hike on the Crest trail, which is approximately 8.2 miles with an elevation gain of 2000 feet and loss of 2700 feet. For those wanting to expend a little more energy.
3. Stampede pass to the Lodge A hike, which is approximately 4 miles. This hike will start near the Stampede Pass Weather Station, and follow the Pacific Crest Trail. Level trail with 600ft.descent through a several acres ripe wild strawberries.
4. Power lines to the Lodge: Same as number 3 except 2 miles Shorter and 25 ft. elevation gain.
5. John Wayne Trail Hike or mountain bike 8 level miles along an abandoned RR line and 2 miles on forest service roads to the Lodge. Approximately 300 ft elevation gain in last mile.
6. Meany Lodge property. Hike from the lodge Length approximately 1/4 mile round trip, 30 ft elevation loss and gain. Hike to view train going into or out of Stampede Pass Tunnel. Good hike for our beginning hikers!
7. Meany Lodge Property. Hike on mountaineer property. Length, approximately 1 mile. Elevation gain and loss 450 ft. Hike to Meany Lodge lookout with a special reward for all children who reach the lookout and wild huckleberries for their parents.
8. Lizzard Lake. For small children, drive to Lizzard Lake and watch the little fish jump out of the water to catch the bugs. Approximately a 4-mile drive one way. Good destination for the very young
