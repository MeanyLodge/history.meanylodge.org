Dog Sled Program

Perhaps you have seen the Dog Sled Crossing signs along the road to Meany Lodge. Maybe during a winter trip to Meany you experienced a sled team pulling along a trail. I had a team pass me this past winter while hiking out to Crystal Springs. At Family Hiking Weekend we will have the opportunity to learn more about the sport of dog sledding and the human and canine athletes that take to the snow.

On Saturday, August 2, Michael and Doreen Johnston will bring their dog team to Meany for a special Family Hiking Weekend program. There will be afternoon demonstrations of summer pulling, and an evening presentation about dog sledding.

For more information about Family Hiking Weekend, contact Brian and Sojin Thompson at 425-228-6412 or bdthom@comcast.net. Sign up is through Brown Paper Tickets. Call 1-800-838-3006, or use the online sign-up.
