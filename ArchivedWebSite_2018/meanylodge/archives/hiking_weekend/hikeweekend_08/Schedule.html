Schedule and Activities

We have great plans for Family Hiking Weekend 2007. As well as the fun hikes and great food, we have a special treat of as Michael and Doreen Johnston bring their dog team to hiking weekend.

Saturday

10:00 a.m. Arrive at Meany find a bed or a camping space

10:30 a.m. Meet in the lodge for information about Saturday and Sunday's hikes, and Hug-a-Tree

11:00 a.m. Organize car pools with leader and depart

1:00 p.m. Stop for lunch on hike

3:00 p.m. Return from hikes and have a snack

3:30 p.m. Dog sled demonstration— Michael and Doreen Johnston and dogs

5:30 p.m. Dinner

6:30 p.m. Dave Agee from BNSF talks to the kids about railroad crossing safety

6:45 p.m. Decorate cookies and play games

7:00 p.m. Dave Agee from BNSF talks to the adults about railroad crossing safety

8:00 p.m. Desert

7:30 p.m. Dog sledding presentation— Michael and Doreen Johnston

10:00 p.m. Lights out

Sunday

7:30 am. Wake up

8:00 am. Breakfast

8:45 am. Pack and clean lodge

9:00 am. Make sack lunch

9:30 am. Depart on Sunday hikes

2:00 p.m. approximately. Leave trailhead for home

For more information about Family Hiking Weekend, contact Brian and Sojin Thompson at 425-228-6412 or bdthom@comcast.net. Sign up is through Brown Paper Tickets. Call 1-800-838-3006, or use the online sign-up.
