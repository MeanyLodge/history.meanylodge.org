Subject: 2011 Mid-Week Work Party

The following is a proposed Mid-week WP schedule (for starters)

  • June - Tuesday June 7 - Thursday June 9 (in lieu of May)
  • June 28 - 30
  • July 12 - 14
  • August 16 - 18
  • September 27 - 29
  • October 11 - 13

Items of work that we may entertain:

  1. Telephone Cabin upgrade
    There is a rumor that the Telephone Cabin wants to be fixed up for the Women's weekend July 8th to July 10th . The seniors could help in this effort by hauling off the junk. This is proposed for the first June WP (7-9) if those interested in cleaning up the cabin were available to haul out the junk and load it. The seniors will likely be involved in other activities and not likely available for general cleaning activities.The painting could come on the second WP in June or some other time of Norm's choosing.
  2. Back hoe
    On the first WP in June it is proposed that we rent a small back hoe to
    • Dig the foundation for the Worm Tow head pole foundations - if we are going to replace the head pole and safety gate poles this year (Mike?)
    • Repair the access road to the lodge
  3. Repair the Pac Rat
    I believe that most of the parts are on hand. The proposal is to repair it for sale and purchase another more updated machine to replace it. The cost difference (buy - sell) could be something in the $8000 area
  4. Tree Removal
    Take down the dead trees near the Tow Hut. One tree will require the services of a professional tree trimmer as it will need to be taken down in parts. When we have him there we could also limb other trees that need it. This will likely cost something in the order of $1000. Chuck do we have your OK?
  5. Stove Hood
    There is a rumor that we need to install a stove hood in the kitchen. If this is the case it will likely involve carpentry work (covering up the old exhaust hole and framing in a new one - Doug?). It will also likely involve electrical work as well as some mechanical. All- is this a project that is going to have to be done and are we going to do it or are others - Chuck?
  6. Other items yet to be determined
