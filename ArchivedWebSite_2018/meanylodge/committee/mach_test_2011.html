2011 Mach Gate Tests

On Saturday April 23, 2011, Dave Clarr, Alex de Klerk, Jim Fahey and Chuck Welter, went up to Meany to test Moch's safety gate and its stopping distance. A series of five tests were performed

  • 2nd Gear brake on
  • 2nd Gear brake on - Second try
  • 3rd Gear brake on
  • 2nd Gear brake off
  • 3rd Gear brake off
Note that the stopping measured distance is from the sack to the resting place of the vertical barber poles when all came to a stop. In reality the stopping distance is much shorter. Each test the gates reacted somewhat differently. Thus this experiment changed more than one variable each time. So measurements are not all equal.

We used a sack tied onto the rope. The sack contained some 50 plus pounds of sand bags and weights from the Moch tow counter balance.

We ran the 2nd gear brake on twice because the first time the safety gates barber poles pulled out of the snow and did not brake the gate plug. Dave sitting in the tow hit the stop button when he noticed the system failed. This led us to the first conclusion: We really need to pin down the bottom of the safety gate some how

After the gate blow out we put the barber poles back in the snow and stomped down and added snow around it especially on the up hill side. The barber poles stayed in place until the last test of 3rd gear no brake.

In viewing the video this morning Jim noticed what appears to me the safety gate plug sliding up the barber poles, the barber poles bending back and the plug wire slipping up the barber pole. Thus the safety gate does not come apart until the sack is about half way up the gate. If this is correct we may want to re think things like;

  • Not using barber poles that flexes so much.
  • Fixing the safety gate plug to the vertical poles in such a way that they can not slide up the pole.
  • Anchoring the base of the barber poles so that they do not come up and out.
