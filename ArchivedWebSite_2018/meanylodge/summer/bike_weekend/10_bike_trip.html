Welcome to the Meany Lodge Bike Trip Page

The first annual Meany lodge Bike trip is almost upon us. What better way to come and see the lodge then by doing a Mountain bike ride. The trip stars at Hyack and follows the scenic John Wayne trail (Old Milwaulkee RR righ of way), then on FR 54, then on FR 420. You will spend the night at Meany and leave the next day. Rain or shine, we meet Saturday July 17th, at 9:30am (we will leave sharply at 10:00). On Sunday gear must be packed and ready to go by 1:00 pm and be picked up at the Hyak parking lot by 4:00pm.

The ride will be on compact gravel roads, with a few hills toward the end, so fat tire bikes only, it's just fine to push up the last few hills and there may be a truck to help those that wish to have it (It will be transporting you gear. for details on distance and elevation, click on Meany Waypoints
.

A day of activitiess is planned at the lodge

  • Expert will give a talk on mountain biking safety.
  • Riding events for kids 7 years and up include:
    • obstacle course
    • challenge ride, and woods race.
  • Evening activities:
    • outdoor barbecue
    • fire side singing
    • bike movies
    • ping pong tournament, and more.
Following Sunday morning breakfast a bike maintenance workshop will take place followed by beginning and advanced rides. Gear will be transported to the Hyak parking lot.

So now that you are all excited about coming - here is what you should plan to Bring:

  • Helmet, Bike tools, spare tube, well oiled chain and full tires!
  • sleeping bag (to be used on bunks)
  • personal effects & cloths (towels, Meany's has very hot water for showers)

And the costs is $45 per persons - and this includes Sleeping arrangements, Showers, Dinner, Breakfast, and a sack lunch.
