The Annual Meany Summer Picnic
August 15th 2009--1:00 pm until ???

Len & Linda Boscarine have generously offered to host our in-town picnic on Saturday August 15th in their beautiful lake front yard on Burien Lake.

This is a great opportunity to re-connect with old friends or make new ones.

We begin a noon and eat about 1:00PM for a pot luck. We will have plates silver ware, water, tea and coffee. If your name is in the front half of the alphabet, please bring a "hot dish". If your in the last half, please bring a cold dish or dessert. It is also a good idea to bring folding chairs.

People are welcome to bring swim suits, water toys and enjoy the water.

Planing to come ? please RSVP by Clicking on Linda
Address: 1600 SW 156th St, Burien.WA 98166
