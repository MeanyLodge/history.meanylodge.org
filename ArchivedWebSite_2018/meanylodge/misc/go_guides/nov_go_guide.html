Winter Sports Season Dec 27 2008 - Mar 9 2009

Season Pass is available!!!! - Go to the Meany website and click on Season Pass link in Nav bar on the left. This is the best deal you can get, and if you buy before December 1st you get a discount, so don't wait!!

So your thinking of skiing or snowboarding this winter?? Well if you can do it in the snow, there is a good chance that we offer lessons on how-to at Meany. For newbies, you can learn Downhill, Cross-country, Telemark &Back country skiing, snowshoeing and snowboarding. For intermediates and above, you can sharpen your skiing/snowboarding skills. For the expert we're even offering a downhill racing clinic (limited to 8). Meany has it all!!

To check out our lesson packages, go to Meany website, then click on Winter Sports Activity in the Nav bar on the left, then click on Sports Programs at the top or program/registration at the bottom. The page you bring up provides a brief description, along with dates and links for registration.

To see what a Meany weekend is like, go to the Meany Website, then click on “Winter Sports Activity” in the Nav bar on the left. To register, simply click on the room/board link on the page.
