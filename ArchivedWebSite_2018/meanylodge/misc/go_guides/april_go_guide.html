What is Happening at Meany In April?

Well in true Meany fashion we are ending the ski season with a bang, Please look at the events planned and come visit !!!!!

Spring Ski Fling April 4 - 6


Join us for one last time this season for skiing and riding on our record level snow. We're going to open Meany the weekend of April 4-6.
We will do the 7:30 PM cat run on Friday night for the first track hounds and JUST ONE cat run Saturday morning at 8:30 AM for the rest of us.

Note there will NOT be a Saturday night or Sunday morning cat run!! If you come you are here for the weekend



The cost* for this grand time is:AdultsChildrenToddler
Friday & Saturday night$70$45free
Saturday night only$55$30free
*Plus Brown Paper Ticket processing fee.

To register for this fantastic event please click on Meany Spring Fling


MOFA at Meany April 11 - 13

If you need your MOFA card, Meany's a great place to get it. Completing the primer First Aid at the club room the week before, you are then ready for the first Meany surprise, the intensive hands-on scenarios that the instructors will put you through. Since there is still snow, you will get your second surprise, realism in doing First Aid in the wilderness. Of course all of this will work up an appetite and the food at meany is the third surprise. Hearty, healthy and cooked from scratch. What is said at Meany is

"If you come away hungry after meals,.....well it was your fault"
