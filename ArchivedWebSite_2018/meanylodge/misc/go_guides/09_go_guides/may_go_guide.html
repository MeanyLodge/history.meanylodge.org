What is Happening at Meany in the summer

Please use the following for age breaks in signing up for any Meany event.

  • Adult = Age 14 +
  • Child = Age 5 - 13
  • Under 5 = Free



Work Parties

It takes a LOT of work to maintain Meany. For example, This year's work parties will replace the 1938 wood stove with a new wood burning furnace; replace the rope tow's mechanical roto-phase drives with solid state ones, and a host of other important projects. Go ahead and click on the Meany Calendar link in the nav bar for the schedule and come join the fun.

Family Hiking Weekend

Family Hiking Weekend (first weekend in August) is a great time to see what Meany is like without snow !!!. please check the link in the nav bar on the left for more info, or if you are ready to come and want to register, please click Hiking Weekend registration
