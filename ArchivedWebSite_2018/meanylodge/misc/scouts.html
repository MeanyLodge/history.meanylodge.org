Welcome to the Meany Scout page

Meany has for many years hosted Boy and Girl Scout troops. This year we just had a Girl Scout Troop snowshow up to the Lodge from the Sno-Park. We are schedule to host a Boy Scout Troop on March 14-16 and a Girl Scout Troop on March 28-30. During theses weekends, not only will the troops ski, but they will also be taught winter survival skills.
