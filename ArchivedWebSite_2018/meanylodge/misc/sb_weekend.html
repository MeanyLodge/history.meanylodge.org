Welcome to the Meany Snowboard weekend page


Come one come all !!!! To the annual Meany Snowboard Festival, held during President's Day weekend. The festival is run by Meany Lodge's own Snowboard Director, Steve Bozorth, and is dedicated to carving out the Meany runs with snowboards. This weekend is all about fun, boards and ski's.
With the return of winter (ie snow), we are looking forward to shaping one or two killer jumps. Get a chance to try out the new terrain devices designed and built by our own Alex DeKlerk. We have located and placed both boxes and our rail. We are looking forward to lots of fresh snow and plenty of good turns. With proper snow we might even have the return of the "cheese wedge tow in"!
Come and get tips from everyone around you. Or sign up for a private lessons with one of our instructors, who will get you to a whole new level in your snowboarding ability.

Steve is highly experienced. He's taught at both Crystal Mountain and Snoqualmie Pass,and has been instructing at Meany for over 8 years. He is committed to the all mountain style that Meany offers.

Join us for food, fun and freshies at Meany Lodge!!
The menu offerings for all the snow shredders and romantics couldn't be better. We will do one of the past favorites, For Saturday morning breakfast, will will have Chef Ray's KILLER Blueberry French Toast and for dinner, Snowboard Director Steve's CHEESE and Mac. Dessert for the evening will be your choice of Smores by the fire with the kids, or for those who are looking forward to a little Valentines Day Romance, strawberries and chocolate for the adults. MMMMM...good!
While the kids are watching snowboarding and snow flicks downstairs, you wil be enjoy your own relaxing evening upstairs with a cheesy romance movie on the big screen. Snow, sweethearts and a chick flick, how can you go wrong?

If you would like more information on the spectacular event, please email Steve at snowboard_director@meanylodge.org or if you are ready to "go for the gold" then click on registration. Once there, select the dates you want to come, (Feb 13-16, Feb 13-15, Feb 14-16, Feb 14-15), or click on day usage to just come of a day.
